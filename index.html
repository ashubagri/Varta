The VARTA promise

Knowledge grows when we think together

VARTA aims to make useful knowledge easier to explore and thoughtful discussion easier to join. These principles guide how we learn and contribute.

Evidence with context

Use reliable references for factual claims where possible. Show uncertainty honestly and correct mistakes openly.

Respectful disagreement

Challenge ideas without attacking people. Make room for different backgrounds, languages and perspectives.

Tradition and science, clearly labelled

Explore cultural and traditional knowledge with care while distinguishing historical accounts, personal beliefs and claims supported by scientific evidence.

Useful, original contributions

Share your own explanations, questions and insights. Credit sources and avoid copied articles, spam and misleading claims.

Safety and privacy

Do not publish private information, harassment or content that puts others at risk. Report content that breaks community standards.
