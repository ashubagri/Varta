Sanskrit, Memory, and Structured Thought

Philosophy

Ancient Indian intellectual traditions did not separate language from logic. Sanskrit was structured with mathematical precision, functioning not merely as a medium of communication, but as an algorithmic framework for memory and grammatical exactitude.

Revisiting these memory traditions today offers fresh perspectives on cognitive science, linguistics, and the architecture of knowledge systems.
