A small collection of the living world's soundscapes.

Pause and listen to birdsong, rain, moving water, wind and forest ambience. Notice how each recording changes in rhythm, texture, intensity and distance. This library brings together freely reusable recordings for learning and mindful listening.

A five-minute listening practice

Listen without rushing to name the sound

  1. Choose one recording and sit comfortably.
  2. Listen once without doing anything else.
  3. Listen again and notice high and low sounds, repetition, pauses and background layers.
  4. Write down three things you heard and one question you would like to investigate.
  5. Reflect on the difference between the measurable properties of sound and your personal experience of listening.

Science connection: Sound is a mechanical wave. Frequency, amplitude, duration and the listening environment influence what we hear. A nature recording can support observation, but it does not by itself prove medical or spiritual effects.

NĀDA connection: Indian contemplative traditions include practices of attentive listening and concepts such as Nāda. These can be explored as philosophical and experiential traditions alongside, but not confused with, acoustic physics.
