VARTA Reference Resources

A practical gateway to free and trusted sources for learning about Bharat and the world—from original Indian texts and cultural archives to public data, science institutions, live weather and satellite dashboards, and official world-affairs information.

Built for students, curious readers, researchers, teachers, creators and community members. Start with original sources whenever possible; use summaries to orient yourself, then verify important claims.

07 · How to use a reference responsibly

Before sharing a claim, ask:
1. Is this an original source, a report about a source, or someone's interpretation?
2. When was it published or last updated?
3. Does the source provide evidence, methods, definitions and uncertainty?
4. Can another reliable source independently confirm the key point?
5. Is the claim historical, cultural, philosophical, spiritual, scientific or political—and is it being presented in the correct category?
6. Is the link freely accessible, and does it permit the intended reuse?

Free to read does not automatically mean free to republish. Respect copyright, licences, privacy and attribution requirements. VARTA does not endorse every position expressed by a linked institution.
