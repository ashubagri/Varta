Focused Force: The Physics of Acoustic Beamforming and Military Sonic Defense Systems

In modern defense architecture, non-lethal deterrence has evolved through the precise manipulation of sound waves. At the heart of this technology lies acoustic beamforming—a wave-interference technique that transforms ordinary sound into a highly directional tactical force.

1. Constructive vs. Destructive Interference

By using a phased array of acoustic transducers and controlling the timing of electrical signals, sound waves combine constructively directly in front of the array to form a high-intensity beam while canceling each other out to the sides.

2. Defense & Security Applications

Systems like Long Range Acoustic Devices (LRADs) project long-range intelligible voice commands, non-lethal area denial tones, and perimeter security deterrents across oceans and crowds.
