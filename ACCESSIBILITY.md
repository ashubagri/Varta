# VARTA Accessibility Statement

VARTA aims to make its knowledge and conversation features usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology or navigate without a mouse.

## Current goals

- Use semantic HTML, meaningful page titles and clearly labelled form fields.
- Keep text readable and provide visible keyboard focus.
- Use accessible status messages for actions such as sign-in, search and form submission.
- Make layouts usable on mobile and desktop.
- Provide descriptive text for meaningful images and controls.

## Known limitations

VARTA is an evolving project. These goals do not mean every page has been audited or that full conformance with WCAG has been verified. Some pages or interactive features may still have keyboard, contrast, screen-reader or responsive-layout issues.

## Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, open a GitHub issue with the page URL, what you were trying to do, and the assistive technology/browser involved if you are comfortable sharing it. Do not include sensitive personal information.
