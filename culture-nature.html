Guide 01 · Water heritage

Stepwells and traditional water knowledge

Stepwells and baolis combined water access, architecture and community life in many parts of India. Their design varied with local geology, climate, patronage and use. They offer valuable heritage lessons, though modern water systems also require contemporary engineering and maintenance.

Guide 02 · Living traditions

Indian festivals: many regions, many meanings

Festivals may bring together seasonal calendars, local histories, religious practice, food, music and community gathering. The same festival can be understood differently across regions and families; documenting those differences is part of preserving living heritage.

Guide 03 · Nature & ecology

Biodiversity begins close to home

Local biodiversity includes native plants, birds, insects, soil life and the habitats that sustain them. Observing seasonal change, protecting water and reducing habitat damage can begin in a neighbourhood, school or community garden.

Guide 04 · Built heritage

Architecture shaped by climate

Courtyards, shaded streets, thick walls, verandas and locally available materials appear in many traditional building systems. Their performance depends on climate, orientation, materials and use; modern design can test and adapt these ideas rather than romanticising every old technique.
