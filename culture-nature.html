Living in Rhythm with the Seasons

Ecology & Heritage

Traditional Indian culture is deeply anchored in ecological rhythms—from agricultural solstices to water conservation rituals like stepwells (baolis). These practices were never purely symbolic; they were sophisticated community blueprints for sustainable coexistence with nature.

Documenting local landscapes and seasonal transitions helps restore a mindful relationship with our environment.
