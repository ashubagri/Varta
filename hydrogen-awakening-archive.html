How each video becomes an article

  1. Discover and catalogue: identify public videos, playlists, titles, descriptions and available captions from the channel.
  2. Understand the original idea: review the source material and connect it with related videos where appropriate.
  3. Research and verify: check scientific, historical and scriptural claims against reliable sources.
  4. Write original articles: explain the idea in a coherent, useful way; do not simply copy transcripts or repeat the same article under different titles.
  5. Preserve attribution: credit Ashu Bagri and Hydrogen Awakening and link to the source video or playlist.
  6. Publish and connect: place each article in the right VARTA section, link related articles and include it in the sitemap.
Editorial promise: The archive will distinguish established scientific evidence, historical evidence, scriptural references, traditional interpretations and personal philosophical ideas. A meaningful symbolic connection will not be presented as a proven scientific fact. Where a video expresses an original hypothesis, it will be attributed as such and examined fairly.
