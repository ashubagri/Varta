# VARTA Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

VARTA is a community for thoughtful conversation and knowledge sharing across science, Indian knowledge, sound, culture, nature and world affairs. We want participation to be welcoming, useful and respectful.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat people with dignity, even when you disagree.
- Discuss ideas and evidence; do not attack people.
- Be honest about facts, uncertainty, sources and personal opinions.
- Respect different backgrounds, traditions and viewpoints.
- Protect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information without permission.
- Share only content you have the right to use.
- Keep posts relevant to the conversation and avoid spam or repeated unsolicited promotion.
- Report harmful content or behaviour instead of escalating disputes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, targeted abuse, hate speech, doxxing, impersonation, spam, malicious links, and content intended to intimidate or endanger others are not acceptable. Disagreement, criticism and good-faith debate are welcome; abuse is not.

## Moderation

VARTA maintainers may review reports and take proportionate action, including removing content, restricting participation or suspending accounts where platform capabilities permit. Decisions should consider context, severity, repetition and user safety.

## Reporting a concern

Please use the repository's Issues feature to report a community or conduct concern without posting sensitive personal information publicly. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. If a report contains private or sensitive information, do not publish it in a public issue.

## Scope

This code applies to VARTA spaces and project interactions, including posts, comments, issues, pull requests and other community channels operated by the project.

## Guiding principle

Be curious, be constructive, verify claims, and leave room for people to learn.
