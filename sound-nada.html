Sound & NADA

Explore Indian music, sound science, mantra traditions and listening practices.

Sound can be studied as a physical wave, an artistic experience and a focus of contemplative practice. These perspectives can enrich one another without assuming that every spiritual claim has already been scientifically proven.

Guide 01 · Practice & philosophy

What is Nāda Yoga?

Nāda Yoga is a family of contemplative approaches that use listening and sound as a focus for attention. Practices differ across traditions. Approach them respectfully, and treat claims about specific health outcomes as questions that require appropriate evidence.

Guide 02 · Acoustics

How sound and resonance work

Sound is a mechanical wave that travels through a medium. Frequency relates to perceived pitch, amplitude to sound pressure and loudness, and resonance occurs when a system responds strongly at particular frequencies. Musical instruments demonstrate these principles in tangible ways.

Guide 03 · Indian classical music

Raga: structure, mood and musical development

A raga is more than a scale: it involves characteristic melodic movements, important notes, phrasing and conventions that guide performance. Hindustani and Carnatic music have rich, related but distinct systems and histories.

Guide 04 · Research & practice

Mantra, attention and meditation

Mantra repetition can serve as an attentional anchor within a spiritual or meditative practice. Research on meditation covers a range of methods and outcomes; it does not establish that every mantra or frequency produces the same measurable effect in every person.
