Privacy

Privacy notice

This page explains the intended privacy approach for VARTA. It is a plain-language notice, not legal advice. It must be kept aligned with the platform's actual code, hosting, database configuration and any third-party services before VARTA invites a wider audience.

Last reviewed: 11 November 2026

Information that may be processed

  • Account information: information needed to create and authenticate an account, handled in part by the configured authentication provider.
  • Contributions: posts, replies, profile details and uploaded media that you choose to submit. Published contributions may be visible to other visitors.
  • Technical and security records: service logs and diagnostic information may be processed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the site.
  • Moderation data: submitted text or metadata may be sent to automated moderation services to assess policy risks. These checks are imperfect and are not a factual verification service.

How information is intended to be used

To operate accounts and publishing features, display contributions, protect the service from abuse, troubleshoot faults, enforce community rules and improve reliability. Information should not be collected for unrelated purposes without appropriate notice and a valid basis.

Public contributions

Do not publish passwords, authentication tokens, private contact details, financial account information or another person's private information. Content you publish may be copied or indexed by others. Removing a post from VARTA may not remove copies or search caches elsewhere.

Service providers and specific data flows

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the public VARTA website. When you visit the site, your browser requests website files from GitHub. Review GitHub's Privacy Statement for its handling of visitor and technical data.
  • Supabase: provides authentication, database and video-storage services. Account details, profile information, contributions, notifications and related service records are handled by the VARTA Supabase project. Access is controlled by the application's database and storage permissions. Review Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI moderation: VARTA sends the text of a post or reply to OpenAI's moderation API for automated safety checks. For video submissions, VARTA sends the title, description, category and source URL for text checks. The moderation function does not send the uploaded video or audio for this check. Moderation results are imperfect and are not factual, copyright or media-content verification. Review OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Translate: some pages load Google's translation widget from translate.google.com. Loading the widget makes a request to Google; if you use translation, page text may be processed by Google to provide the translated version. Review Google's Privacy Policy.

Published posts, replies, profile details and published videos may be visible to visitors. Pending video uploads are stored in a private bucket until publication, subject to the application's access rules. Do not submit information you would not want processed by the relevant provider or displayed publicly.

VARTA's stated policy is not to sell members' personal data. Providers may process information to deliver their services, and information may be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect the service, subject to applicable law.

Retention, security and your choices

VARTA does not yet publish a complete, verified retention schedule. Retention depends on the data type, operational needs, provider configuration and applicable law. Use the account controls available in the product to manage your profile where supported. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact the site operator through the current VARTA contact profile and describe the request without sending passwords or secrets. The operator will need to verify the request and assess applicable legal and operational requirements.

Children and sensitive information

Do not use VARTA to post sensitive personal information or information about children. The operator should publish age-appropriate rules and comply with applicable privacy and child-safety requirements before enabling access for children.

Updates and important limitation

This notice may change as features are added. Before commercial launch, the operator should review the actual data flow, cookie/analytics use, moderation provider terms, user deletion process, applicable Indian law and any requirements in the countries served. Do not treat this page as proof that all legal or technical compliance steps are complete.
