In Vedic philosophy, the first subtle element to emerge is Akash (Space or Ether). The defining characteristic of Akash is Shabd (Sound). Space itself is defined by its ability to hold and transmit vibration; sound is the very fabric of space.

2. From Vibration to Matter: The Panchamahabhutas

As primordial vibration differentiates, it condenses into the five great elements in sequential order, each inheriting previous qualities: Akash (Space) -> Vayu (Air) -> Agni (Fire) -> Apas (Water) -> Prithvi (Earth).
